The Cult Behind the Craft

Ill-Fated Idols is a small studio making eldritch, Lovecraftian creatures in colors that were never meant to be seen. Each idol is a 3D-printed and hand-painted technicolor nightmare, with some featuring LED lighting writhing within.

Every idol begins as a digital sculpt by the incredibly talented NS Miniatures. We bring it into the physical world, printed layer by layer, then finished by hand. From spectral resin that appears to contain an ever-shifting ancient being, to hand-painted celestial entities, every idol has a story to tell.. if you're willing to heed their call.

  • 3D-printed in durable resin
  • Hand-painted, one at a time
  • Vivid, translucent colorways
  • Made to order — yours alone
